The story behind Faith Éclat

It started with a discovery.

Faith Éclat wasn't created from a business idea. It started with a personal experience.

During my time in Pakistan, I became friends with a Pakistani family in my neighbourhood. One of the women introduced me to this cream and showed me how she used it. I decided to try it for myself — and I was pleasantly surprised by the way my skin looked and felt, especially the appearance of pigmentation and pimple marks.

"Then I shared it with the women in my family — my mother, sister, aunt and a close friend. Their experiences encouraged me to share it beyond my own family."

That little discovery became Faith Éclat. And now, I'm bringing that experience to you.

Your skin comes first

Safety, allergies & returns.

Everyone's skin is different. Please take these steps seriously, even if you've never experienced an allergy before:

  1. Patch-test the product before your first use — a small amount on your hand or behind your ear.
  2. If you experience irritation or a suspected allergic reaction, discontinue use immediately.
  3. Speak to your own doctor or a qualified healthcare professional for advice on how to proceed.
  4. If you'd like to request a return on this basis, keep your medical documentation — you'll need it.

FAQ

A few common questions.

How should I use it?

After cleansing your face, apply a small amount to clean, dry skin and gently massage it in at night. A little goes a long way.

Can I use it during the day?

Faith Éclat is designed for night use. During the day, maintain your skin with your usual moisturizer and broad-spectrum sunscreen, especially when going outdoors.

When will I see results?

Every person's skin is different. Some people notice changes sooner, others need more time and consistent use — results are individual and cannot be guaranteed within a specific number of days.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, always — even if you've never experienced an allergy before. Apply a small amount behind your ear or on your inner arm and monitor it before applying to your face.
